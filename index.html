About Us

What is Interact?

🤝

Service

We plan and lead hands-on service projects — cleanups, literacy drives, donations — that solve real community needs in Oroquieta and beyond.

👑

Leadership

Every member leads. From project chair to club president, you get real experience in organizing teams, budgets, and partnerships with Rotary.

🌍

Fellowship

More than meetings. We build friendships, celebrate World Interact Week, and connect with Interactors across the Philippines and the world.
